# Code Of Conduct

## Our Standards

Project spaces should be welcoming, respectful, and focused on constructive technical work.
Examples of positive behavior include giving actionable feedback, acknowledging uncertainty,
crediting others, and helping newcomers understand the contribution process.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory or sexualized language, threats,
doxing, deliberate disruption, and publishing another person's private information.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other public
spaces where someone is representing this project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, reject contributions, temporarily restrict participation,
or permanently ban participants when behavior is harmful to the community. Enforcement decisions
should consider context, severity, prior behavior, and the safety of affected people.

Use GitHub's report or block controls for urgent platform abuse. For project-specific concerns,
contact the maintainer privately through their GitHub profile and do not post sensitive details in
a public issue. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

This policy is adapted from the principles of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
